# Acceptable Use Policy

Privé exists to give **people** a private, calm place for **their own** conversations.
This policy explains what that means and what the project will not support.

## Privé is for

- Reading and replying to **your own** accounts, on **your own** computer.
- Keeping several of your accounts (personal, work, family…) in one place.
- Studying, auditing, translating and improving the software.

## Privé must not be used for

1. **Spam or bulk messaging** — unsolicited, mass, automated or marketing messages, including "broadcast"
   workarounds and contact-list blasting.
2. **Harassment or abuse** — threats, stalking, intimidation, hate, or contacting people who asked you to stop.
3. **Surveillance of others** — connecting accounts you do not own or are not authorized to use, monitoring a
   partner, employee or child without consent, or covertly exporting someone else's conversations.
4. **Scraping and data harvesting** — collecting phone numbers, profiles, group members or messages at scale.
5. **Fraud and impersonation** — phishing, scams, fake identities, or misleading people about who you are.
6. **Evading enforcement** — circumventing a network's bans, rate limits or anti-abuse systems.
7. **Illegal content or activity** — anything illegal where you or the recipients are, including
   child sexual abuse material, which is reported to authorities wherever possible.

## What the project commits to

To make misuse harder and keep Privé a personal tool, the official project **will not add or accept**:

- Bulk send, mass broadcast, scheduled campaigns, auto-replies to strangers, or message templates for marketing.
- Contact or group-member scraping and export tools.
- A headless "server mode" or public API meant to run Privé as a messaging gateway.
- Features designed to hide activity from the networks' anti-abuse systems.

Features are evaluated with one question: *does this mainly help a person talk with people they know,
or mainly help someone send messages at scale?* Only the first belongs in Privé.

## About the license

Privé's code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE), which lets anyone use, modify and redistribute it.
An open-source license cannot restrict *how* the code is used, so this policy cannot legally bind every fork.
It does govern:

- the official Privé project, its releases, issue tracker and community spaces;
- which contributions are accepted;
- the use of the **Privé name and logo** (see [`docs/brand/BRAND.md`](docs/brand/BRAND.md#10-using-the-brand)):
  forks built for prohibited uses must not use them.

Violations of the networks' terms remain between the user and the network.

## Reporting misuse

If you see Privé (or something using its name) being used for abuse, open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/baddiamond/prive/security/advisories/new) or an issue
with the `abuse` label. Do not include personal data of victims in public issues.
