Three directions for the mark

Each must work at 16 px in a menu bar, in one color, and on light and dark. All three are drawn on a 64-unit grid with 7-unit strokes.

A Sealed bubble

A speech bubble with a keyhole: messaging that stays locked to you.

+ Instantly readable.
− Keyholes and bubbles are common; hard to own. The keyhole fills in at 16 px.

B Convergence

Many accounts flowing into one calm place: the unified inbox as a symbol.

+ Tells the "all in one" story.
− Says nothing about privacy. Too detailed for 16 px and relies on color.

C The accent Recommended

A lowercase "p" whose bowl is a chat bubble and whose stem is its tail, crowned by the acute accent of privé.

+ Ownable: the accent is our signature everywhere (wordmark, focus, "sent" tick). Survives 16 px and one color.
− Privacy is told by the name and the product, not the drawing.
