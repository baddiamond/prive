privé
Brand guide · v0.1 draft
Essence

Your conversations,
at home.

Privé brings every conversation into one calm, fast place that lives only on your computer. No account, no servers in the middle, nothing to sell.

The feeling we aim for
Feels like A quiet room with good light
A sealed envelope
A well-kept notebook
A key that only you hold
Never like A social feed fighting for attention
An ad-supported app
A corporate dashboard
A noisy notification center
Five promises
01 Local by design Everything stays on your computer. Delete one folder and it is all gone.
02 Yours, no sign-up No Privé account, no tracking, no telemetry. Ever.
03 One calm place Every account and network speaks the same simple language.
04 Fast and light Opens instantly, stays out of the way, respects your battery.
05 Open to everyone Open source, accessible, multilingual, built with its community.
Personality Discreet/ Calm/ Quick/ Honest/ Welcoming Name: French for "private". Written Privé in text, prive in code.
